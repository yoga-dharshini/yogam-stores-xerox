A custom domain and hosting account were not supplied, so deployment cannot be completed safely without choosing a domain and obtaining access to the DNS/hosting account. Do not invent a domain in `sitemap.xml` or `robots.txt`.

After the domain is selected:
1. Replace `YOUR-DOMAIN.example` in `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.
2. Set the domain to serve this folder over HTTPS.
3. Verify the live site and mobile layout.
4. Add the domain as a Google Search Console Domain property and verify ownership via DNS.
5. Submit `/sitemap.xml`.
